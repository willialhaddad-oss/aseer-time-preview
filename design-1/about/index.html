Aseer Time x Papa Kanafa Vaughan

About us

Two brands under one roof in Vaughan: Aseer Time for fresh juices and viral desserts, and Papa Kanafa for kanafa and churros. All of it made fresh, right in front of you.

From Kuwait to Vaughan

  1. 2011Aseer Time opens its first shop in Kuwait. Aseer means juice in Arabic.
  2. 2014Papa Kanafa, its sister brand, opens in Kuwait.
  3. 2023The first Aseer Time shop in Canada opens in Mississauga.
  4. 2025Aseer Time x Papa Kanafa opens in Vaughan on August 16.

Aseer Time

A juice and dessert brand from Kuwait, now with more than 200 branches around the world. In Vaughan it makes fresh juices, layered fruit cocktails, smoothies, milkshakes, crepes, mini pancakes, waffles, ice cream and the Dubai chocolate collection.

Papa Kanafa

The kanafa brand of the same group, known for giving traditional kanafa a new twist. In Vaughan it makes kanafa soft or crispy, with cheese or ashta cream filling, and churros in a cup with a dipping sauce.

Our shop in Vaughan

We are a franchise of the international Aseer Time brand. Find us at 53-9100 Jane St, 2 minutes from Vaughan Mills and near Canada's Wonderland.
